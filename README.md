# DUSIB Patwari Prep

Study site for the DUSIB Patwari paper (Post Code 14/26): the subject-specific syllabus, (A) Knowledge of Relevant Acts and (B) Computer Knowledge.

- **Delhi Laws**: every Act in the syllabus with quick facts, section tables and exam traps (DUSIB 2010 with the 2026 amendment, DD Act 1957, Delhi Land Revenue Act 1954, Delhi Land Reforms Act 1954, NCT Laws (Special Provisions) 2011, LA Act 1894, RFCTLARR 2013, Punjab Land Revenue Act 1887, land reforms in India, Delhi administration).
- **Computer**: revision tables for all 13 syllabus topics.
- **Syllabus Extras**: Delhi Governance & Civic Institutions (PDS & NFSA, MCD reunification, Imperial Delhi Committee, vigilance, DBSE, Master Plan 1961–81, Parks & Gardens Society, Delhi Jal Board, magistrate functions), Delhi Govt schemes, and computer memory, operating systems, browsers, search engines and email.
- **Delhi GK**: facts, who's who, history, the seven cities, monuments, geography, culture, institutions, transport, sport and 2025–26 updates.
- **1,463 MCQs** in practice or test mode, a **timed 100-question mock** with −0.25 negative marking, and a **Revise** list that collects every wrong answer and every question you save.

One page, no build step, no tracking. Works offline and installs to the home screen.
Fonts (Inter, Literata, IBM Plex Mono) are self-hosted under the SIL Open Font License.
